# Comprehensive Acoustic Reference for 2D Numerical Breast Phantom Modeling (0–10 cm)

---

## 1. Overview & Architectural Scope

This reference defines a **breast-only 2D numerical acoustic phantom** on a Cartesian grid of dimensions $(N_x, N_y)$ with lateral step $dx$, axial depth step $dy$, and configurable total domain depth $D = N_y \cdot dy \in \{4, 6, 8, 10\}\text{ cm}$ measured from the anterior skin surface ($z = 0$) to the posterior breast boundary ($z = D$). The domain terminates strictly at the posterior edge of the retromammary fat; deeper anatomical depth classes ($8\text{ cm}$ and $10\text{ cm}$) represent larger breast geometries rather than deeper thoracic penetration.

1. **Acoustic Material Parameterization:** Independent assignment of longitudinal speed of sound ($c$, $\text{m/s}$) and mass density ($\rho$, $\text{kg/m}^3$) across macro- and micro-structural breast compartments, yielding characteristic acoustic impedance $Z = \rho c$ ($\text{MRayl}$).
2. **Anatomical & Depth-Class Geometry:** Configurable breast-size classes ($4, 6, 8, 10\text{ cm}$) comprising optional coupling standoff, epidermis/dermis skin layers, subcutaneous fat lobules, a branching fibroglandular parenchyma cone with Cooper's ligaments, and a terminal retromammary fat plane.
3. **Non-Elliptical Lesion Morphology:** Parametric Fourier-radial contour generators with Gaussian spicules and stiffness-biased cellular growth models calibrated to quantitative ultrasound (QUS) morphometric solidity targets, replacing legacy elliptical approximations.
4. **Multi-Scale Acoustic Heterogeneity:** Correlated Gaussian random field (GRF) parenchymal texture ($\ell = 0.21\text{ mm}$) paired with sub-wavelength scatterer distributions for pulse-echo speckle synthesis.

**Scope Exclusions & Evidence Governance:** Acoustic attenuation ($\alpha$), shear-wave elasticity, and all chest-wall or intrathoracic structures (retromammary fascia as a distinct reflective layer, pectoralis major/minor, intercostal muscles, costal cartilage, ribs, parietal pleura, and lung) are excluded from the domain; simulation grids extending beyond $z = D$ must terminate with a perfectly matched layer (PML) or homogeneous fat half-space. Quantitative parameters prioritize **2020 or later primary studies `[P]` and reviews `[R]`**, explicitly tagging pre-2020 legacy fallbacks **`[L]`**, engineering assumptions **`[A]`**, and numerical calibrations **`[C]`**.

## 3. Deep-Dive Modeling Enhancements

### 3.1 Lesion Placement, Size & Non-Elliptical Contour Modeling

* **Anatomical Localization & Population Mix:** Based on 3D ultrasound tomography (UST) of $298$ masses (Littrup et al., 2021), baseline pathology sampling weights are **$78 : 105 : 91$** (malignant : fibroadenoma : cyst) **`[P]`**, with malignant cases split $80 : 20$ between IDC and DCIS **`[A]`**. Lesion centers must be constrained to the **fat–glandular interface (FGI)**—pixels adjacent to both adipose and fibroglandular labels—for **$95\%$ of cancers** ($74/78$), **$63\%$ of fibroadenomas** ($66/105$), and **$36\%$ of cysts** ($33/91$; remaining $64\%$ inside fibroglandular tissue) **`[P]`**.
* **Depth & Size Sampling:** Sonographic tumor-to-skin distance is **$8.6 \pm 4.4\text{ mm}$** ($n = 130$) and tumor-to-nipple distance is **$41.1 \pm 30.0\text{ mm}$** **`[P]`**. Sample lesion center depth from a left-truncated normal $\mathcal{N}(8.6, 4.4^2)\text{ mm}$ constrained so the entire contour remains at least $3\text{ mm}$ below the skin and above $z = D - t_{\text{rm}}$; **do not scale lesion depth with $D$**, as larger breasts primarily expand the deep posterior lesion-free zone **`[A]`**. Remove legacy $20\text{ mm}$ upper size caps (since $20.6\%$ of IDCs in Gity et al., 2021 exceed $20\text{ mm}$): sample screening malignant diameters from a truncated log-normal distribution with mean $13\text{–}16\text{ mm}$ (range $5\text{–}36\text{ mm}$), or $31 \pm 16\text{ mm}$ for symptomatic cohorts **`[P]/[A]`**.
* **Non-Elliptical Contour Parameterization (Generator A):** Because ellipses enforce unit solidity and represent only benign shapes, generate 2D lesion contours in polar coordinates $(r, \theta)$ before applying depth-to-lateral anisotropy scaling $\eta$ and random orientation $\psi$:
  $$r(\theta) = R_0 \left[ 1 + \sum_{k=2}^{K} \alpha \, k^{-p} \, u_k \cos(k\theta + \phi_k) \right] + \sum_{j=1}^{n_{\text{sp}}} h_j \exp\!\left(-\frac{\Delta\theta_j^2}{2w^2}\right)$$
  where $u_k \sim \mathcal{U}(0.5, 1)$, $\phi_k \sim \mathcal{U}(0, 2\pi)$, $h_j = h \cdot \mathcal{U}(0.5, 1)$, spicule angles $c_j \sim \mathcal{U}(0, 2\pi)$, and $r(\theta) \ge 0.2 R_0$ **`[R]/[A]`**. Alternatively, **Generator B (2D Stiffness-Biased Growth)** seeds a TDLU pixel and iteratively recruits 8-connected neighbors with probability $p(c) \propto S(c)^{-\gamma} (1 + \beta \cdot n_{\text{occ}}(c))$, where local tissue stiffness $S$ ranks $\text{ligaments} > \text{gland} > \text{fat}$, forcing lesions to deform organically around Cooper's septa **`[P]/[A]`**.
* **Morphometric Calibration Targets (`[C]`):** Calibrate Generator A on **solidity** ($A / A_{\text{convex}}$) rather than perimeter-sensitive convexity, matching Pan et al. (2024) clinical ultrasound benchmarks (benign $0.98 \pm 0.01$, malignant $0.88 \pm 0.06$; Zhang et al., 2022 malignancy threshold $\le 0.92$):
  * *Smooth / Oval Cyst:* $\alpha \in [0.02, 0.06], K \le 4, \eta \in [0.60, 0.95] \implies \text{Solidity} = \mathbf{1.000 \pm 0.000}$.
  * *Lobulated Fibroadenoma:* $\alpha \in [0.15, 0.20], p = 0.5, K = 5, \eta \in [0.50, 1.00] \implies \text{Solidity} = \mathbf{0.963\text{–}0.983}$.
  * *Irregular Carcinoma (no spicules):* $\alpha \in [0.25, 0.35], p = 1.0, K = 10 \implies \text{Solidity} = \mathbf{0.928\text{–}0.961}$.
  * *Spiculated IDC:* $\alpha = 0.10, K = 10, n_{\text{sp}} = 8, h = 0.35, w = 0.12 \implies \text{Solidity} = \mathbf{0.884 \pm 0.027}$.

### 3.2 Rare, Subtle & Micro-Structural Features

* **Peritumoral Hyperechoic Halo:** Applied exclusively to IDC lesions as an irregular **$1.5\text{–}2.5\text{ mm}$ morphological dilation annulus** that conforms to the outer spicule contour rather than a smooth ring **`[L]/[A]`**. Interior pixels alternate stochastically between tumor/collagen acoustic values and surrounding adipose values to model desmoplastic infiltration and acoustic impedance contrast.
* **Microcalcifications:** Modeled as $1\text{–}3\text{ px}$ ($0.1\text{–}1.0\text{ mm}$) point inclusions ($c \approx 3200\text{ m/s}$, $\rho \approx 2800\text{ kg/m}^3$) within DCIS/IDC regions, reflecting the **$70\text{–}90\%$** calcification detection prevalence in DCIS **`[R]/[L]`**. Suppress microcalcifications entirely when grid spacing $dx > 0.3\text{ mm}$ to avoid sub-pixel aliasing artifacts.
* **Sub-Resolved Ducts & Vessels:** Modeled explicitly as tubular branches ($0.5\text{–}2.5\text{ mm}$) on high-resolution B-mode grids ($dx \le 0.25\text{ mm}$), or merged into bulk adipose/fibroglandular compartments on coarser USCT grids ($dx > 0.25\text{ mm}$) **`[P]`**.

### 3.3 Reconciliation of Conflicting Literature Values

* **Invasive Tumor Density ($945$ vs. $1070\text{ kg/m}^3$):** Li et al. (2022) report $\rho = 945 \pm 20\text{ kg/m}^3$ (inherited from Sanchez et al., 2017), whereas Pawlowska et al. (2022) specify $1070\text{ kg/m}^3$, and mammographic phantom materials (Bliznakova et al., 2024) use X-ray printing densities ($0.93\text{–}1.04\text{ g/cm}^3$) unsuitable for acoustics. **Selection Rule:** Sample $\rho \in [1045, 1070]\text{ kg/m}^3$ when simulating positive impedance contrast ($Z_{\text{tumor}} > Z_{\text{gland}}$), or $\mathcal{N}(945, 20^2)\text{ kg/m}^3$ when reproducing the Li et al. (2022) USCT benchmark **`[A]`**.
* **Cooper's Ligament SOS ($1457$ vs. $1750\text{ m/s}$):** Partial-volume averaging across thin collagenous septa ($0.2\text{–}0.5\text{ mm}$) lowers effective sound speed on coarse grids. **Selection Rule:** Assign $1457 \pm 18.5\text{ m/s}$ when $dx \ge 0.25\text{ mm}$ (USCT scale) and $1750\text{ m/s}$ when $dx \le 0.25\text{ mm}$ (high-frequency B-mode scale) **`[P]`**.
* **Spatial Correlation Length ($\ell = 0.21\text{ mm}$):** Earlier notes misattributed Table III texture standard deviations ($28.8 / 30.4\text{ m/s}$ and $18.22 / 20.82\text{ kg/m}^3$) as millimeter correlation lengths. In Li et al. (2022), the true isotropic correlation length is **$\ell = 0.21\text{ mm}$** for both $c$ and $\rho$ **`[P]`**.
* **Temperature & Measurement Modality Consistency:** Do not mix *ex vivo* room-temperature values with *in vivo* values within the same simulation; maintain the Li et al. (2022) parameter set as the unified baseline and use *in vivo* bulk SOS measurements (Bezek et al., 2025; Anzola et al., 2025) strictly for macro-scale validation.

### 3.4 Macro-Scale Heterogeneity & Micro-Scale Speckle Formulations

* **Macro-Scale Parenchymal Texture (`[P]`):** Superimpose independent zero-mean Gaussian random fields $\delta c(\mathbf{x})$ and $\delta \rho(\mathbf{x})$ with an isotropic Gaussian covariance kernel onto the adipose and fibroglandular masks:
  $$C(r) = \sigma^2 \exp\!\left(-\frac{r^2}{2\ell^2}\right), \qquad \ell = 0.21\text{ mm}$$
  with standard deviations $\sigma_c = 28.8\text{ m/s}$, $\sigma_\rho = 18.22\text{ kg/m}^3$ for adipose tissue (truncated at $\pm 0.9\sigma$) and $\sigma_c = 30.4\text{ m/s}$, $\sigma_\rho = 20.82\text{ kg/m}^3$ for fibroglandular tissue (clamped to Table 2 min–max bounds). At $dx = 0.20\text{ mm}$, $\ell \approx 1\text{ px}$ (near-white pixel noise), whereas at $dx = 0.05\text{ mm}$, $\ell \approx 4.2\text{ px}$ (spatially resolved speckle texture).
* **Micro-Scale Pulse-Echo Speckle (`[L]`):** For full-wave pulse-echo simulations, populate solid tissue compartments with sub-wavelength point scatterers at spatial density $\approx 2\text{ scatterers}/\lambda^2$ carrying a $\pm 3\%$ random perturbation in mass density $\rho$. Keep simple cyst interiors strictly free of sub-wavelength scatterers so they exhibit realistic anechoic posterior enhancement.

### 3.5 BI-RADS Density Parameterization & Depth Size-Class Tiers

* **ACR/BI-RADS Composition & Global SOS Validation:** Parameterize parenchymal composition via adipose area fraction $K_{\text{fat}}$ **`[L]`**, and validate the resulting assembled phantom against *in vivo* conventional-transducer bulk sound speeds measured by Bezek et al. (2025) ($n = 100$ women) **`[P]`**:
  * **BI-RADS A (Almost entirely fatty):** $K_{\text{fat}} \approx 95\%$ $\longrightarrow$ Bulk SOS target **$1459.6 \pm 0.74\text{ m/s}$** (area-averaged map sits near $\sim 1440\text{ m/s}$ due to pulse-echo vs. path-average differences).
  * **BI-RADS B (Scattered fibroglandular):** $K_{\text{fat}} \approx 85\%$ $\longrightarrow$ Bulk SOS target **$1475.6 \pm 15.92\text{ m/s}$**.
  * **BI-RADS C (Heterogeneously dense):** $K_{\text{fat}} \approx 66\%$ $\longrightarrow$ Bulk SOS target **$1515.6 \pm 27.10\text{ m/s}$**.
  * **BI-RADS D (Extremely dense):** $K_{\text{fat}} \approx 40\%$ $\longrightarrow$ Bulk SOS target **$1545.7 \pm 20.62\text{ m/s}$** (denser breasts also exhibit smaller geometric semi-axes: $a_{1t} \sim \mathcal{TN}(4.20, 1.23^2)\text{ cm}$ vs. $5.85\text{ cm}$ for types A–C; Li et al., 2022).
* **Depth Size-Class Tiers (`4, 6, 8, 10 cm`):** Because no post-2019 study establishes a universal mapping from bra cup size to posterior depth—and supine/prone positioning reduces breast volume by $\sim 25\%$ relative to standing ($375 \pm 148\text{ cc}$ vs. $495 \pm 204\text{ cc}$; Macek et al., 2024)—model **$4\text{ cm}$** (small/compressed), **$6\text{ cm}$** (average uncompressed), and **$8\text{ cm}$ / $10\text{ cm}$** (extrapolated large-breast tiers) using explicit geometric thicknesses rather than cup labels **`[P]/[A]`**.
